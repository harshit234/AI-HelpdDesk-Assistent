Escalation Procedure – IT Helpdesk
Document ID: POL-ESC-002
Version: 1.8
Last Updated: 2024-10-01
Owner: Helpdesk Manager: Priya Sharma

PURPOSE
This procedure defines when and how to escalate tickets that are at risk of SLA breach, involve multiple teams, or require management visibility.

ESCALATION TIERS

Tier 1 – L1 Support (First Contact)
Team: Helpdesk Analysts (Karan Mehta, Sneha Joshi, Akash Patel)
Handles: Password resets, basic hardware faults, standard software installations, account unlocks, VPN connectivity for standard users.
Escalate to Tier 2 when: Issue cannot be resolved within 30 minutes, issue requires admin-level access, or the user is a C-level executive.

Tier 2 – L2 Support (Technical Support)
Team: Systems Engineers (Deepak Rao, Fatima Sheikh)
Handles: Server-side issues, network faults, application errors, database connectivity, firewall rules.
Escalate to Tier 3 when: Root cause is unknown after 2 hours of investigation, the issue involves data loss, or production systems are impacted.

Tier 3 – L3 Support (Expert / Vendor)
Team: Platform Engineers (Vikram Iyer, Nandita Kulkarni) and external vendor contacts.
Handles: Infrastructure failures, cloud platform issues, critical security incidents, and any issue requiring vendor engagement.
Escalate to IT Head (Rajesh Nair) when: P1 SLA is at risk, a data breach is confirmed or suspected, or a fix requires budget approval above Rs. 50,000.

ESCALATION STEPS
1. Update the ticket with a clear summary of steps already taken and the reason for escalation.
2. Change the assigned group in the ticketing system to the target tier group.
3. Send a direct message (not just an email) to the receiving engineer to ensure awareness.
4. For P1 escalations, also call the on-call number: +91-98XXXXXXXX (internal directory: ext. 1100).
5. Notify the affected user's manager if downtime exceeds 1 hour.

MANAGEMENT ESCALATION MATRIX
Duration Unresolved | Action Required
> 2 hours (P1)      | Notify IT Head by phone
> 4 hours (P2)      | Notify Helpdesk Manager (Priya Sharma)
> 24 hours (P3)     | Notify Team Lead and log in weekly report
> 3 business days (P4) | Review in weekly triage; notify requester

DO NOT SKIP TIERS
Engineers must not bypass Tier 2 and go directly to Tier 3 without documented justification. Skipping tiers delays resolution and prevents proper knowledge capture.
