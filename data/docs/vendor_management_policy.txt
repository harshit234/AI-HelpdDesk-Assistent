VENDOR INCIDENT PROCESS
1. When a vendor outage is suspected, verify the issue is on the vendor's side before calling (check their status page if available).
2. Open a ticket in the helpdesk under the relevant vendor category with the circuit ID or contract number in the subject.
3. Call the vendor NOC and get a reference/case number. Add this to the ticket.
4. Update the ticket every 30 minutes with the vendor's status update.
5. If the vendor breaches their own SLA, document it. Rajesh Nair reviews vendor SLA breaches quarterly for contract renegotiation leverage.

THIRD-PARTY ACCESS
Any vendor requiring remote access to our systems must be given a time-limited contractor account (see POL-ACC-008) and must connect only via our monitored VPN. Vendor access sessions must be observed by an internal engineer. Unaccompanied vendor access is not permitted.
