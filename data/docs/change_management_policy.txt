Change Management Policy
Document ID: POL-CHG-005
Version: 1.5
Last Updated: 2024-08-10
Owner: IT Operations – Rajesh Nair

PURPOSE
All changes to production infrastructure, applications, and security configurations must follow this policy to prevent unplanned outages and maintain an auditable change history.

CHANGE CATEGORIES

Standard Change
Definition: Pre-approved, low-risk, repeatable changes with a documented procedure. No CAB approval required.
Examples: OS patching via approved patch schedule, routine certificate renewals, adding a standard user account.
Process: Log the change in the CMDB before execution. Post a completion note within 1 hour of finishing.

Normal Change
Definition: Any change not classified as Standard or Emergency. Requires Change Advisory Board (CAB) review.
Lead Time Required: Minimum 5 business days before the proposed change window.
CAB Meeting: Every Tuesday at 3:00 PM IST. Chaired by Rajesh Nair.
Process: Submit Change Request (CR) form in the ticketing system with: scope, rollback plan, impact assessment, and test results. CAB either approves, rejects, or requests more information.

Emergency Change
Definition: A change needed to resolve an active P1 or P2 incident where delay would worsen impact.
Process: Verbal approval from Rajesh Nair is sufficient to proceed. A full CR must be submitted retrospectively within 24 hours. Emergency changes are reviewed in the next CAB meeting.
Risk: Emergency changes account for the majority of change-related outages. Always document the rollback plan even under time pressure.

MAINTENANCE WINDOWS
Scheduled maintenance windows: Every Saturday 11:00 PM to Sunday 3:00 AM IST.
Extended window (quarterly): Last Saturday of March, June, September, December — 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM IST.
No changes outside these windows without CAB approval or Emergency Change designation.

ROLLBACK POLICY
Every change, including Standard changes, must have a rollback plan documented before execution. If a change cannot be rolled back (e.g. data migrations), this must be explicitly stated and approved by Rajesh Nair personally.

CAB MEMBERS
Rajesh Nair (Chair), Priya Sharma, Vikram Iyer, Deepak Rao, and one representative from the affected business unit.
