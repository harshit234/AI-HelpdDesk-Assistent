Security Incident Response Policy
Document ID: POL-SEC-006
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2024-11-01
Owner: IT Security – Nandita Kulkarni

PURPOSE
This policy defines how to identify, report, contain, and recover from security incidents including data breaches, ransomware, phishing attacks, and unauthorised access.

WHAT QUALIFIES AS A SECURITY INCIDENT
- Any confirmed or suspected unauthorised access to systems or data
- Malware or ransomware detected on any company endpoint or server
- Phishing attack where credentials were entered on a fraudulent site
- Unusual bulk data export or exfiltration attempt
- Any vulnerability exploited in production

IMMEDIATE STEPS (FIRST 15 MINUTES)
1. Do NOT attempt to remediate on your own. Contain first, fix later.
2. Isolate the affected system: disconnect from network if possible (do not power off — this destroys forensic evidence).
3. Report immediately to Nandita Kulkarni (ext. 3301) and Rajesh Nair (ext. 1001). Do not use email if you suspect email is compromised — use phone or Signal.
4. Do NOT notify the affected user's broader team or post in general Slack channels. Information about security incidents must be on a need-to-know basis until assessed.
5. Open a P1 ticket in the helpdesk system under category "Security" with subject "SECURITY INCIDENT – [brief description]." Mark it as restricted.

CONTAINMENT
Nandita Kulkarni leads containment. Steps depend on incident type:
Ransomware: Immediately isolate all systems on the same VLAN. Verify backup integrity before any recovery attempt. Contact cyber insurance provider (policy number: HDFC-CYB-20241100) before paying any ransom.
Phishing with credential entry: Force password reset for the affected account. Revoke all active sessions. Check audit logs for access in the past 48 hours.
Unauthorised access: Revoke access immediately. Preserve logs — do not clear or rotate until forensic review is complete.

NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
If personal data of customers or employees is involved: Legal and Compliance must be notified within 6 hours. CERT-In notification is required within 6 hours under the DPDP Act if the breach involves more than 500 individuals' data.
External communication (press, customers): Only by the CEO or authorised spokesperson. IT must not communicate externally without approval.

POST-INCIDENT
Mandatory PIR within 48 hours for all security incidents. Nandita Kulkarni chairs the review. All findings and remediation steps are logged in the Security Register (restricted access).
